2. Where the 13 TeV analysis does fit angle-sensitive observables, it pays the full price to make that legitimate. The W-mass fit observable (muon pT, Î·, charge) is itself a convolution of ptV, yV and decay angle â same "leakage" mechanism as yours, exploited deliberately.
